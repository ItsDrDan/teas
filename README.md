# TEAS Study Hub + Scrub In, by ItsDrDan

Free ATI TEAS 7 prep for future nurses: Reading, Math, Science and English.

- **index.html: TEAS Study Hub.** Section pages for every unit, with a 3-minute **Learn it** lesson per unit; **Flashcards** (400+); a Daily 5 (easy → hard); a week-by-week study plan; a **progress report** with a score chart against the 62% and 75% cut scores (printable); a **test-day checklist**; a score estimator; Central Valley nursing program requirements; topic search.
- **practice.html: Scrub In.** Quick Drill, **My Missed Questions** (spaced review at 1, 3 and 7 days), Reading Passages, Timed Set, Mini Practice Test, Full Practice Test (170 questions, real timing) and a Must-Know Sheet. All TEAS question types: multiple choice, select all that apply, fill in the blank, ordered response and **hot spot** (tap the diagram), plus the on-screen four-function calculator. Pacing feedback after every timed set and test. Deep links: `practice.html?section=sc&unit=2`, `?mode=full`, `?mode=missed`.
- **Works offline / installs to a phone home screen** (manifest.webmanifest + sw.js).
- **Anonymous stats** go to the shared ItsDrDan Game Stats sheet through games/track.js (game "teas": topic and right/wrong only; no names or answers).
- **data/:** Daily 5 pool, search index, and lessons/flashcards (learn.json).
- **assets/:** logos, mascot, icons, badges, app icons and the social preview image.

Every question, passage and diagram is original and written from ATI's public TEAS 7 content outline, with science background from free OpenStax textbooks.
